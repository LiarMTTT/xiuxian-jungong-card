# 白玉鎏金灵铳提示词组 v0.2 审批稿

> 状态：已批准并归档；用于替代已经失败的 v0.1 长提示词
>
> 原则：先锁二维美术与整体造型，再锁准镜几何，最后统一成图；禁止一条提示词同时承担所有任务
>
> 批准日期：2026-08-14；批准的是提示词结构与验收流程，不代表任何已有生成图合格

## 一、为什么不再使用旧写法

旧提示词的问题不是词不够多，而是同时要求技术页、多视图、复杂机械、材质、构图与精确几何。模型把“二维动画感”降成次要要求，最终回到三维产品渲染，并把大量否定词重新画进图里。

新提示词遵守四条规则：

1. 每次只解决一个设计层；
2. 正向描述短于负面清单，负面只保留最危险的错误；
3. 形体用一个母题反复发展，不堆仙侠名词；
4. 准镜轴线必须由几何底稿提供，不能只靠文字祈祷模型画对。

## 二、阶段 A：枪身造型稿

这一阶段暂不画法阵准镜，先判断它是否真正摆脱“现代枪换玉石纹理”。

### A1 中文生产提示词

```text
二维中国仙侠动作游戏的角色专武立绘，手绘线稿、线条粗细变化、干净的大块赛璐璐明暗、带动画关键帧般的轮廓张力；拒绝照片写实和三维产品渲染。

只画一把原创制式灵铳，左后三分之四英雄视角，深靛蓝纯净背景。它不是现代步枪换材质，而是把玉圭、如意弧与仙剑剑脊重新组织成肩射灵械：玉圭式枪托、如意负空间、两片白玉环抱的机匣核心、向前收束的仙剑式护脊、干净空膛的玉环枪口。枪托、机匣、护木和枪口反复使用同一道月牙—如意曲线，形成单一、流畅、完整的整体轮廓。

主体是温润、近乎不透明的羊脂白玉结构大面；鎏金仅作为少量连续的结构收边与阵路；乌金只用于枪栓、扳机、锁止和活动关节；紫色灵光仅从玉体薄边与少数阵枢中内蕴透出。华丽来自比例、负空间、层叠玉片和材质秩序，不使用金饰贴花与满版雕花。高规格二次元仙侠游戏 CG，漂亮、灵动、整体性强、具有可作为产品线母版的清晰设计语言。
```

### A2 English production prompt

```text
Premium 2D Chinese-xianxia action-game weapon key art: hand-drawn tapered linework, deliberate line-weight variation, clean large cel-shaded shadow shapes, and the energetic silhouette of an animation key frame. Flat illustrative color design, not photographic realism and not a 3D product render.

Show exactly one original standard-pattern shoulder-fired spirit weapon in a rear-left three-quarter hero view against a clean deep-indigo background. Do not reskin a modern rifle. Build the entire silhouette from one coherent shape family derived from a jade gui tablet, a single flowing ruyi curve, and the spine of an immortal sword: a ceremonial jade stock with ruyi-shaped negative space, two embracing jade shells around the action, a long sword-spine fore-end, and a restrained jade-ring muzzle with a visibly empty bore. Repeat the same crescent-ruyi curve through stock, receiver, fore-end, and muzzle so the object reads as one designed artifact.

Use broad, warm, nearly opaque mutton-fat-white-jade structural masses. Gilt appears only as a few continuous structural edges and formation paths. Dark spirit iron is limited to the bolt, trigger, locks, and actual moving joints. Subtle violet spirit light glows only beneath thin jade edges and at a few formation nodes. Luxury comes from proportion, negative space, layered jade planes, and material hierarchy—not jewelry or decorative clutter. Beautiful, lively, cohesive anime game CG with a strong reusable product-family identity.
```

### A3 极短负面词

```text
photorealistic 3D render, modern rifle reskin, ivory plastic, transparent crystal body, wood, gold jewelry appliqué, baroque ornament, steampunk, giant energy tube, dragon-head muzzle, muzzle obstruction, multiple views, text
```

### A4 验收

- 缩成缩略图后，轮廓仍能与现代枪明显区分；
- 第一眼是二维仙侠游戏立绘，而非三维白模渲染；
- 白玉是结构体块，不是表面纹理；
- 枪托、机匣、护木、枪口确实共享同一道形体语言；
- 枪口为空，无任何东西从枪管内部伸出。

任一项失败，不进入阶段 B。

## 三、阶段 B：准镜结构编辑

输入必须包含：

1. 已通过阶段 A 的枪身图；
2. 一张人工绘制的简洁几何底稿，明确枪管轴 `A`、瞄准轴 `S`、共同消失点、前后框圆心、机匣左侧基座与右侧退壳禁区。

不允许再让模型凭文字猜测准镜位置。

### B1 中文编辑提示词

```text
保持参考图中枪身的轮廓、比例、材质、视角和二维手绘游戏 CG 风格不变，只增加一套法阵准镜。严格服从几何底稿，不重新设计枪身，不改变枪管方向。

准镜由两个小型、完全相同的 3/4 圆形白玉开环构成。两个框具有相同尺寸体系、厚度、缺口方向和鎏金阵纹；后框只因透视而略大。后框圆心、前框圆心与枪身透视共同指向底稿标出的同一个消失点；两个框平面彼此平行，并同时垂直于瞄准轴。框内为空，只显示轻薄法阵准星；无玻璃、无镜筒，两个圆心之间无杆、无线、无光束。

支架只从机匣左侧面升起：左侧低矮基座向上伸出两根彼此独立的如意形短悬臂，再向内挑起，将前后阵框悬置在枪管正上方。支架不跨过机匣顶部，不接触枪管，不遮挡右侧退壳口和拉栓路径。活动枢轴小而精致，以乌金关节和少量紫色阵纹表达自适应校准，不使用裸露齿轮塔。

删除几何底稿中的辅助线。最终仍是一张完整、漂亮的二维仙侠游戏武器立绘。
```

### B2 English edit prompt

```text
Preserve the approved weapon's silhouette, proportions, materials, camera, and hand-drawn 2D anime-game rendering. Add only the formation sight. Follow the supplied geometry scaffold exactly; do not redesign the weapon and do not change the barrel direction.

Use two small, identical three-quarter-open circular white-jade formation rings. They share the same profile, thickness, gap direction, and gilt rune language; the rear ring is only slightly larger through perspective. The rear-ring center, front-ring center, and weapon perspective converge on the single vanishing point marked by the scaffold. Both ring planes are parallel to each other and perpendicular to the sight axis. Their apertures are empty except for delicate projected formation reticles: no glass, no scope tube, and no rod, wire, beam, or line between the two centers.

Mount the sight only from the LEFT FACE of the receiver. A low side plate supports two separate short ruyi-shaped cantilever arms that rise and bend inward, suspending the rings above the bore. Nothing crosses the top of the receiver; nothing touches the barrel; the right-side ejection port and bolt path remain fully open. Use small dark-spirit-iron pivots and restrained violet calibration runes, not exposed gear towers. Remove all construction guides in the final image.
```

### B3 极短负面词

```text
square sight, mixed ring shapes, giant rings, off-axis rings, sideways rings, top bridge, barrel mount, blocked ejection port, center connecting line, scope tube, gear tower, changed weapon body, 3D render, text
```

### B4 验收

- 后框中心、前框中心与枪身透视共用一个消失点；
- 两框是同款小型 3/4 圆环，不是一大一小两种法器；
- 左侧基座、向上再向内的悬臂关系一眼可读；
- 顶部、枪管和右侧退壳／拉栓路径均为空；
- 两框之间不存在任何实体或发光连接线。

任一项失败，直接废弃，不进入阶段 C。

## 四、阶段 C：最终主视觉统一

阶段 C 只整理画面，不允许再次重画结构。

### C1 中文编辑提示词

```text
严格保留参考图已经批准的枪身轮廓、枪口、左侧准镜支架、前后阵框位置与共轴关系，不增加、删除或移动结构。

把整张图统一成高规格二维中国仙侠动作游戏的武器主视觉：强化有粗细变化的手绘轮廓线、干净的大块赛璐璐阴影、白玉暖光、克制鎏金边线和少量紫色灵光拖尾；用深靛蓝背景和一条顺着枪身向前流动的淡紫灵气弧加强动画张力。保持玉体大面干净，减少细碎纹理；让枪托、机匣和剑脊式护木形成一个清楚、优雅、可记忆的整体剪影。

只显示一把完整武器，无文字、无技术视图、无局部小窗。
```

### C2 English edit prompt

```text
Preserve every approved structural fact in the reference: weapon silhouette, empty muzzle, left-side sight mount, front and rear ring positions, and their coaxial alignment. Do not add, remove, or move components.

Unify the image as premium hand-painted 2D Chinese-xianxia action-game weapon key art. Strengthen tapered expressive linework, large clean cel-shaded shadows, warm white-jade light, restrained gilt edge lines, and a few violet spirit-energy accents. Use a deep-indigo background and one subtle violet energy arc flowing forward with the weapon to create animation-like momentum. Keep the broad jade planes clean and make the stock, receiver, and sword-spine fore-end read as one memorable silhouette.

Show one complete weapon only, with no text, no technical views, and no inset panels.
```

### C3 极短负面词

```text
photorealistic PBR, 3D product render, noisy surface texture, excessive glow, gold clutter, transparent plastic, altered geometry, extra components, multiple views, text
```

## 五、通用变量槽

母版通过后，其他节点仅替换以下变量：

```text
[节点名称]
[武器用途]
[操作方式]
[供能模块]
[核心轮廓变体]
[主灵光颜色]
[专属功能构件]
```

不得替换：

```text
二维手绘游戏 CG
同一母题统领整体造型
白玉主体／鎏金结构线／乌金活动件／克制灵光
功能结构不得被装饰遮蔽
一张图只展示一件灵械
准镜使用几何底稿而非纯文字猜测
```

## 六、失败样本不得反向污染

- 此前所有写实、三维、技术页、多视图、木质、黑枪贴金、白枪贴玉纹、巨大阵框、顶部支架与枪管异物图均为负面样本；
- 不得将任何失败图作为后续图生图底图；
- 阶段 A 必须从零生成；阶段 B 只以通过的 A 图与人工几何底稿为参考；阶段 C 只以通过的 B 图为参考；
- 每个阶段均需单独审批，不因提示词正确而自动通过。
